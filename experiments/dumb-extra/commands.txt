######## Expected compile cost and run times (estimates, not measured on this code)
# Basis: DBRD on 4 v4 chips at 3.74 steps/s with 96 rows of up to 512 tokens, ~184k tokens/s forward and backward in
# float32 (masking/dumb/logs/653000--dbrd--seed42.log, epochs 2-3); SICK-NL's first epoch there took 12 min 37 s for
# 18 steps, ~42 s of compile per new batch length. Without padding to a multiple every batch length compiled again,
# hence the ~47 minutes of the first DBRD/SICK-NL fine-tune per task and LR on a worker.
# Here every batch is padded to a multiple of 128, so a task compiles at most 4 train and 4 eval shapes: 128 and 256 for
# nearly all token-task batches (512 only for the few batches with a cut CoNLL-2002 sentence), 384 for nearly every
# squadnl train batch (79% of its windows are shorter, but a batch of 32 almost always holds a full one). Expected: ~5
# minutes of compile on the first job of each task on a worker. The program depends on the task, the LR and the
# number of updates, not on the model or the seed, so later jobs of that task on that worker hit the compile cache
# (JAX_COMPILATION_CACHE_DIR=~/dumbx/jaxcache). A smoke test (other updates) does not fill it for the real runs.
# Per seed, at an update of 32 (8 x 4 chips) and ~0.03 s of overhead per step assumed:
#   squadnl         90,653 train windows, 2,832 steps per epoch of 32 x 384: ~14 min of training, ~1 min of evaluation
#                   (9,808 dev windows per epoch, 1,251 test), ~1-2 min of loading: ~17 min (51 min per spec line)
#   conll2002       15,812 rows, 494 steps per epoch of 32 x 128: ~1.5 min of training: ~3 min with loading (9 per line)
#   pos-lassysmall  13,817 rows, 431 steps per epoch: ~3 min;  pos-alpino  12,289 rows, 384 steps per epoch: ~3 min
# Per model and worker once: conversion (--params-only, 0.92 GB in the same region) and verify_ckpt.py, ~3-5 min.
# spec_653k.txt: workers 0 and 1 ~120 minutes of jobs, 2 and 3 ~114, plus 2-3 conversions and the first compiles:
# ~2.5 hours for all 72 jobs.
# BLiMP-NL per model: 219,150 masked copies (3.5M tokens) in ~6,850 rows of 512, ~108 batches of 64 rows, forward
# only, float32: ~1-2 min of forward, ~2-3 min of compile, ~1-2 min to read the params and pack: ~5-7 min; the 4 lists
# of step 4 ~30 minutes.
